# GharBhandar 🏡💊
### Household Inventory, Tablet Tracker & AI Bill Scanner

**GharBhandar** is a modern, responsive web application designed for Indian households to effortlessly track kitchen staples, spices, groceries, and daily medications, generate smart shopping lists, and automatically update stock by scanning paper receipts or bill photos.

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **Complete Household Stock Manager**:
   - Pre-loaded with Indian household categories: *Kitchen Staples & Dal/Rice*, *Spices & Wellness*, *Dairy & Breakfast*, *Cleaning & Toiletries*, *Medical & Supplements*, and *Home & Utilities*.
   - Instant `+` and `-` quantity buttons tailored to each unit (`kg`, `g`, `L`, `packs`, `pcs`, `bottles`).
   - Visual stock status bars and one-click **Restock** buttons.

2. **Daily Medicine & Tablet Tracker**:
   - Dedicated pharmacy box to track tablet strip counts.
   - Filter by schedule: **☀️ Morning**, **🌤️ Afternoon**, **🌙 Night**, or **🚨 SOS / As Needed**.
   - Quick **Take 1 Tablet** button and **+1 Strip** quick-add.

3. **Smart Restock List & WhatsApp Sharing**:
   - Automatically compiles items running low or out of stock.
   - Add custom items on the fly (e.g. *Fresh coriander*, *Milk packets*).
   - One-click **Copy WhatsApp List** formatted with emojis for easy family sharing.

4. **AI Bill & Receipt Scanner**:
   - Upload receipt photos or paste order text from Kirana stores, DMart, Reliance Smart, or Apollo/MedPlus pharmacy bills.
   - Automatically matches and increments inventory and medicine quantities.

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Free)

1. Create a free account on [GitHub](https://github.com).
2. Create a new public repository named `gharbhandar`.
3. Upload `index.html` and `README.md` to the repository.
4. Go to repository **Settings** > **Pages**.
5. Under **Build and deployment**, set the branch to `main` (or `master`) and click **Save**.
6. Open your live URL on any smartphone or laptop in your home!
